# Changelog

All notable changes to LMQS (Linux Mint Quick Settings).

## 1.1.0 — 2026-11-24

### New
- Button shape (Appearance › Look): Pill (GNOME default), Square, Squircle, Teardrop, Rhombus or Window, with live preview cards in your accent colour.
- Tile style (Appearance › Look): Long tiles (GNOME), Big tiles (icon above the name, one per column) or Small tiles (icon-only round buttons, as many as fit across).
- Show names on tiles (long and big tiles): turn tile names off for icon-only tiles; names show on hover.
- Big tiles keep the arrow for menus; on small tiles, right-click opens the menu (right-click works on every tile).
- New tiles: Files, Firefox, Google Chrome, Brave, Spotify, Steam, LocalSend, Bitwarden, Android Studio, Calculator, Driver Manager, Ulauncher, Proton VPN, Emote, LibreOffice, Show Desktop, Trash (with item count and Empty Trash), Accessibility and Startup Applications. Like other extra tiles, they start in the hidden tiles.
- What's New page in About, with this changelog.

### Fixed
- Long names wrap onto two lines, centred next to the icon (or under it on big tiles), instead of being cut off with "…".
- Tile rows per page only offers row counts your tiles actually fill.
- Keyboard light at login (Layout › Extras). Keep the keyboard backlight off at start-up, remember how you left it, or leave it to the laptop. Also applied after waking from suspend.

## 1.0.0 — 2026-11-24

First release.

- GNOME-style Quick Settings menu for Cinnamon: pill tiles, round buttons, sliders and slide-out submenus.
- Tiles for Wi-Fi, Wired, Bluetooth, VPN, Power Mode, Night Light, Dark Style, Do Not Disturb, Caffeine, Keyboard, Refresh Rate, Microphone, Camera, Auto Rotate, Hotspot, Touchpad, Airplane Mode, Screenshot, Terminal, Update Manager, NVIDIA Settings and Discord.
- Custom tiles: open an app, a folder or website, or run your own on/off commands.
- Pages of tiles you can rearrange in edit mode, with a paged hidden tiles tray.
- Volume, microphone and brightness sliders with output and input pickers.
- Notifications with a compact Android-style tile row, and a media card with a big player view.
- Panel icons, battery styles, battery time remaining and network speed in the panel.
- Settings window with live preview, accent colours, light/dark styles and reset to defaults.
- Replaces Mint's Sound, Network and Battery applets; uninstall puts them back.
